# Email Templates for ShipLog Outreach

## Philosophy
- **Value First:** Send the preview immediately. No "can I send you X?". Just "Here is X."
- **Low Friction:** No login required to see the preview.
- **Respectful:** Acknowledge they are busy maintainers.
- **Personalized:** Mention specific repo details if possible.

## Subject Lines
1. "Automated changelog for {{repo_name}} (Preview)"
2. "I generated a changelog for {{repo_name}} using AI"
3. "Better release notes for {{repo_name}}?"
4. "ShipLog preview for {{repo_name}}"

## Sequence 1: The "Value Drop" (Cold Outreach)

**Subject:** Automated changelog for {{repo_name}} (Preview)

Hi {{maintainer_name}},

I'm a big fan of {{repo_name}}. I noticed you guys ship frequently!

I'm building **ShipLog** (shiplog.io), an AI tool that turns commit history into readable changelogs for different audiences (devs, stakeholders, marketing).

I took the liberty of generating a preview for your latest release to show you what it looks like:

👉 **[View {{repo_name}} Changelog Preview]({{preview_link}})**

No sign-up needed to view. I'd love to know if this quality would be useful for your release process.

Best,
Captain
Founder, ShipLog

---

## Sequence 2: The "Follow Up" (3 days later)

**Subject:** Re: Automated changelog for {{repo_name}} (Preview)

Hey {{maintainer_name}},

Just bumping this in case it got buried.

Did the changelog preview for {{repo_name}} look accurate? We're tuning the AI to better understand context, so any feedback (even "it sucked") is valuable.

Link again: {{preview_link}}

Cheers,
Captain

---

## Sequence 3: The "Magic Link" Invitation

**Subject:** Your private invite to ShipLog

Hi {{maintainer_name}},

We're opening up a few spots for our beta. Since {{repo_name}} is a perfect fit, I've generated a magic link for you to claim the repo and set up automation:

[Claim {{repo_name}} on ShipLog]({{magic_link}})

Let me know if you have questions!

Best,
Captain
